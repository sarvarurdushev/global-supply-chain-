# Audio licences

Every sound the Nepal 2015 briefing can play, where it comes from, and on what terms.
Rule: **if a licence is unclear, the asset is not bundled.** Nothing below is a film
soundtrack, a track taken from a video site, a recording of a real person's voice, or a
clone of one. Why these choices were made: [`NEPAL_2015_AUDIO_RESEARCH.md`](NEPAL_2015_AUDIO_RESEARCH.md).

## Not bundled

Film and TV music, YouTube Audio Library tracks, Pixabay music (no redistribution as a
standalone file), Free Music Archive tracks without a per-track check, any cloud TTS
output, and every voice model with a non-commercial or unclear licence (Coqui XTTS-v2,
F5-TTS weights, research-only Piper voices).

## Re-rendering

```
KOKORO_DIR=<dir with model.onnx and <voice>.bin> NARRATION_PYTHON=<python with misaki, onnxruntime, soundfile> \
  node scripts/build-narration.mjs            # renders missing clips, rewrites the manifest, prunes unused clips
node scripts/build-narration.mjs --check      # CI-friendly: fails if any clip is missing or stale
```
